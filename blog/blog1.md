# What Data Science Means to Me

I think learning data science in social and interdisciplinary contexts matters because there are a lot of ethical decisions that go into how data is collected, analyzed, and presented. A lot of the time, someone can take the same data and word it differently to make it support two completely different points. Because of that, I think it is important for data scientists to present information truthfully and make sure they are not misleading people with the results.

Analyzing human data can also help us understand things that we might otherwise miss. There is a huge amount of data being created every day through phones, websites, businesses, healthcare systems, and many other sources. There are also constantly new types of data being created. Without data scientists to organize and analyze all of this information, we could miss patterns or important findings that could potentially improve or even save people's lives.

At the same time, there are many things that can go wrong when working with data about people. One major problem is that data can easily become skewed. For example, if data is only collected from one area or from a certain type of population, the results might not accurately represent the general population. A business could then use those results to make a claim about its product even though the data does not represent everyone. This is why it is important to understand where data comes from before making conclusions.

I also think data scientists have a responsibility to respect people's privacy. They should not misuse people's personal information, sell information in ways people did not agree to, or use data in ways that violate people's rights. This becomes especially important in areas like healthcare. Good healthcare data can help clinics and hospitals understand patients better and possibly save lives, while inaccurate or biased data could lead to negative outcomes.

I became interested in data science because I enjoy math, data, coding, and science, so it combines a lot of things that already interest me. My mom is also a medical writer, which has helped increase my interest in using data and science in areas that can help people. In the future, I would like to use what I learn to make a positive difference. Throughout this course, I especially hope to understand more about the ethical side of data science and how to use data responsibly when it involves real people.
